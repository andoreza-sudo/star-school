Whistlestop privacy policy

Whistlestop: Kids Math & Reading. Effective November 20, 2026.

In short: Whistlestop has no accounts, no ads and no analytics. A child's progress is stored only on the tablet. A child's voice never leaves the tablet. We do not collect, sell or share personal information.

Who we are

Whistlestop is an offline learning app for children from Pre-K to 6th grade: math, reading, Spanish and discovery practice, with game time earned by learning. It is made for families.

What stays on the tablet

  • Each child's progress (name or nickname, grade, stars, pet, stickers, answers and settings) is stored only in the app's storage on the tablet. It is never sent to us or to anyone else.
  • The Grown-ups PIN and the optional picture passwords stay on the tablet.
  • Voice: recordings made in the app (Spanish "Say it!" practice and any voice clips a grown-up records) are stored only on the tablet and are never uploaded. Speech recognition is used only when the tablet can do it on the device itself. "Say it!" recordings are deleted automatically.

What we do not do

  • No accounts or log-ins.
  • No advertising of any kind.
  • No analytics, tracking, crash reporting or advertising identifiers.
  • No chat, no sharing with other users, no location.
  • Children never see a price or a purchase prompt. Buying is only behind the Grown-ups PIN.

The only times the app uses the network

  • Getting the app and its updates. The app downloads its own files (the app itself, updates and recorded voices) from whistlestoplearning.com. Nothing about a child is sent with these requests.
  • Google Play billing (the Google Play app only). The one-time "unlock everything" purchase is handled by Google Play. We never see payment details or the Google account. Google's own privacy policy applies to the purchase.
  • Purchase acknowledgement (the Google Play app only). After a purchase, the app sends the purchase token (and the product name, unlock_all) to our own server at whistlestoplearning.com/api/ack, which asks Google Play to confirm the purchase. Nothing else is sent, and we do not store the token.

Our website is hosted by Vercel. Like any web host, Vercel keeps request logs (the IP address and the browser's user agent of each request) for a short time to run and protect the service. We do not use them to identify anyone, and we add nothing about a child to them.

Things a grown-up may choose to do online

Some Grown-ups features are optional and need the internet: sharing or saving a backup file, sharing or printing a report card, and the one-time download of Spanish listening for the tablet's own speech recognition. The app shows a note next to these buttons. A backup or report card goes only where the grown-up sends it, using the tablet's own share or print screen; it never comes to us. The Spanish listening download comes from the tablet's own speech service and sends no recordings.

Children

Whistlestop is designed for children and follows the Google Play Families policy. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children or anyone else.

Deleting data

Everything is on the tablet, so it is in your hands: Grown-ups, then Start over erases everything, a child can be deleted in Grown-ups, and uninstalling the app removes all of its data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
