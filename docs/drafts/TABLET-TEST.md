# Whistlestop: real-tablet test (one page)

For the owner. Do this on two or three cheap Android tablets **that have Google Play**, one of them with **2 GB of memory**
(the box or Settings > About tablet says "RAM 2 GB"). About 30 minutes a tablet. You do not need to understand why a step
is there; just do them in order and write down what you see.

**Before you start:** the app is on the internal testing track, your Google account is a tester and a licence tester, and the
tablet is signed in to that account. Have Wi-Fi on. Have a second device ready to film the screen if something looks wrong.

## The steps, in order

| # | Do this | It is right when |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Install.** Open the internal-testing link on the tablet, install from Google Play, open Whistlestop. | It opens full screen. **No address bar, no "Running in Chrome" strip** at the top. If you see either, stop and tell Claude: the app is not linked to the site and the free sample is not on. |
| 2 | **First run.** Time from tapping the icon to the "Hi! Let's get you ready" screen. Type a name, pick an egg, pick a grade, tap Let's go. | Under about 10 seconds on Wi-Fi. When the keyboard is up, can you still see the name box and reach Let's go? |
| 3 | **First lesson and round, as your child.** Learn > Math > the blue "Learn with ..." button > Let's learn. Then Practice. Open the chest. | The pet's voice is clear and starts right away. No button is cut off. A sticker, stars and game time appear. Time it: lesson + round should be about 5 minutes. |
| 4 | **Wait 3 minutes with Wi-Fi on** (the app is saving its voices), then **turn on airplane mode**. Close the app (swipe it away) and open it again. | It opens to Home with the same name and stars. Play one more round: the voice still talks. Open the Game Room and start two games. |
| 5 | **Games.** First Grown-ups > Tablet speed > turn on "Show the speed box". Then play each of the six games for a minute: 4x4 Hill Climb, Coloring, Block Builder, Star Rally, Snack Stand, Sky Hop. | The box in the corner says 30 fps or more and "worst" under 100 ms. No game freezes, goes black or throws you back to Home. Go in and out of Block Builder and Hill Climb five times each. |
| 6 | **Sound and recordings.** Tap the speaker on a question. In Spanish, try a "say it" stop (it asks for the microphone). In Grown-ups, record one of the "Your voice" lines and play it back. | Android asks for the microphone once. Your recording plays. Music is not too loud over the voice. |
| 7 | **The lock.** Finish 5 Math stops (or tap "Already know it? Test out!"). Tap the 6th stop. | "Not open yet. This stop opens when a grown-up unlocks Whistlestop." and OK. **No price, no Buy button** anywhere a child can reach. |
| 8 | **Grown-ups and the PIN.** Home > Grown-ups. Answer the question, choose a PIN. Go out, come back, try a wrong PIN, then the right one. | A wrong PIN does not open. The right one does. |
| 9 | **Test purchase** (Wi-Fi back on). Grown-ups > Unlock Whistlestop > Unlock or restore > PIN. | The sheet shows the price from Google Play. Google's own payment sheet opens and says it is a test card. After paying: "Whistlestop is unlocked for every child on this tablet." The 6th stop opens. **Five minutes later** check in Play Console that the order is not refunded. |
| 10 | **Saves survive.** Close the app and open it. Then **restart the tablet** and open it. Then leave it overnight and open it. | Name, stars, stickers, the PIN and the unlock are all still there each time. |
| 11 | **Turn, Back and Home.** Turn the tablet upright and back in a lesson and in a game. Press Back (or swipe in from the edge) on Home, in a round and in a game. With the music on, press the tablet's Home button in the middle of a game. | Nothing is cut off. Write down exactly what Back does in each place (we expect it may close the app). After Home, **the music and voice stop** within a second or two; opening the app again shows the same game or Home, not a blank screen. |
| 12 | **Long play, 2 GB tablet only.** Let a child (or you) play 20 minutes without closing: lessons, then games, then lessons. Switch to another app and back twice. | It never restarts by itself and never loses the round's stars. |

## Write down, for each tablet

- Make and model, Android version, memory (GB), screen size.
- Seconds for step 2; minutes for step 3; the fps and "worst" numbers for each game in step 5.
- Every step that was not "right", with **what screen you were on and what you tapped just before**.
- Anything a child could not do alone, any text they could not read and nobody read to them, any wait over 3 seconds.
- In Grown-ups > App log: copy or photograph anything listed there.

## How to send it

Photos of the screen or a screen recording are fine (Android: swipe down twice > Screen record). Put them with your notes in
one message or one folder, named by tablet ("onn 7 - step 5.mp4"). You can also dictate the notes; Claude will sort them.

## Amazon Fire tablets

Fire tablets (Fire 7, Fire HD 8, the Kids editions) are the most common cheap children's tablets, and **they do not have Google
Play**. The Play app cannot be installed on them, and Whistlestop's free sample and the $9.99 unlock exist only in the Play app.
What that means for you:

- A family with only a Fire tablet cannot buy Whistlestop at launch. Say "for Android tablets and phones with Google Play" in the
  listing and on the website, so nobody finds out after asking.
- The web address (app.whistlestoplearning.com) does open in the Fire's Silk browser, fully unlocked and free. Today the website
  does not offer it. That is your decision already on record (HANDOFF section 2 and BEST-APP item 6); Fire owners are the people
  most likely to go looking for it.
- Selling on Fire means a second listing in the Amazon Appstore with Amazon's own payment system. That is real work (new
  packaging, new purchase code, a second review). Decide after launch, from how many people ask.
- Do not test on a Fire tablet for this round, and do not count one as one of the "two or three".
