Every "Now" text was checked against the app as it is today (v196); the few that differ are marked "Check". To answer, reply "approve all", or "approve all except W12, W30", or rewrite any line and send it back. For Section A, give the option you choose for each (for example "D1 yes, P2 option 2"), or say "as recommended".
