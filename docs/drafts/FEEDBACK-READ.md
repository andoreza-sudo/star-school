# Tell us what you think: read-through (FEEDBACK-4)

BEST-APP roadmap item 4. A new card in Grown-ups, behind the PIN, after "About Whistlestop" and before "Grown-ups PIN".

How it works:

- **Email us** opens the tablet's e-mail app with a message to hello@whistlestoplearning.com. The subject holds only the app's version and build. It never holds the child's name, grade or progress: a child's data never leaves the tablet. The grown-up writes the message.
- **Review on Google Play** opens Whistlestop's page on Google Play. It shows only in the Google Play app. The web version shows the e-mail only.
- The line under the title shows the address, so a tablet with no e-mail app still has a way to write.
- Plain links only. The app makes no network call. No child screen ever asks for a rating.
