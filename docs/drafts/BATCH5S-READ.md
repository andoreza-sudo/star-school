# Batch 5s read-through: lessons for Spanish and Discover

12 lessons, in map order: the Spanish map (Hola Plaza to Las Frases), then Discover
(Nature Trail). Each is what the child hears: the six beats, the pet's reasons
when it is caught, and tonight's question for you.

Spanish is the same map for every grade, so its lines are written to suit every age
(the little one, the older kid and the teen-like pet all say them). Discover's Nature
Trail is Kindergarten to 2nd grade: the little one.

Spanish words in a line are written «la vaca»: the app says them in the Spanish voice.
The app fills in each {slot}: in Spanish {a} is an el word and {b} a la word the child
sorted; in Discover {a} and {b} are two things of the child's guess; {ans} is the
pet's answer. The app also says each question and its choices itself.

Until you approve a lesson, it runs on short hand-written lines
(its idea, a Name it line, the pet's reasons), so it works now.

The lesson shapes. Spanish: listen to four pictures and sort them into el and la, say
a word with the pet (out loud: nothing is recorded), hear a word and tap its picture,
pick the right words for a picture, and catch the pet saying "el vaca" or "el mano".
Discover: predict (sort four pictures, any guess is fine), look (what really happens),
explain (pick the reason), and catch the pet thinking small things float, a car is
alive, or the shade melts ice as fast as the sun.

To change a word: edit the JSON in that lesson's file in docs/drafts/.
When you are happy, say so, or run:

`node tools/author.js --approve all --batch 5s --by <your name>`

(or name the lessons instead of all). Approved lessons use their words at the next build.
No lesson of another batch is waiting.
